Tiri collects nothing.

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your files, terminals and conversations stay on your Mac, except for the one thing below you can choose to turn on.

Updates

Every few hours, while your Mac is plugged in and idle, Tiri asks tiri.build whether there's a newer version. Turn off Look for updates in Settings → General, and Tiri never goes online by itself.

Downloads

The site counts each download by day and version. Nothing about who: no IP address, no cookie.

Reporting a problem

Report a Problem fills in a GitHub issue. You read it, and you decide whether to send it.

Titles for past conversations

Apple Intelligence gives past conversations their titles, on your Mac. If you give Tiri your own API key instead, short excerpts go to that provider to make the titles, under your account and nowhere else. The key stays in your Mac's keychain.

Your agents

Claude Code, Codex and Grok talk to their own services, as they would without Tiri.

Questions? Ask on GitHub.
