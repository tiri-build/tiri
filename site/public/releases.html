What's new in Tiri.

Newest first. Tiri updates itself, and your changes stay through every update.

0.5.0

13 November 2026

Write to your agent in a real box, and point at what's wrong.

  • Write to your agent. ⌘↩ in a terminal opens a box at its foot for words, pictures, files and long pastes. If the agent asks something meanwhile, your message waits.
  • Notes on anything. Click the + beside a line, or press Point and click an element of a page or a spot on a picture or PDF, and say what's wrong.
  • Notes go with your message. They wait in the box, where you can read each one or leave it out. A note made by pointing carries a picture with the place marked.
  • A note leads back to its place. Each has a numbered mark where it was made, and sent notes get a tick once the agent has changed their lines.
  • Choose the toolbar's buttons in Settings → General.
  • An agent changing Tiri looks at what it made and checks it against what you asked before it calls it ready.

0.4.0

12 November 2026

Go back to any project, bring your editor's setup, and share a change with a friend.

  • Open Recent. ⌥⌘O, the File menu and an empty workspace list where you've been working: workspaces you closed, and folders you used in Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code or Codex, without the clutter.
  • Close a workspace and come back to it. Its tabs return, and its agents pick up their conversations.
  • Bring in your Cursor or VS Code setup in one step: theme, fonts, wrapping and shortcuts. Undo puts it back.
  • Share a change you made to Tiri as one file. Your friend sees what it changes, and it stays off until they turn it on.
  • Drag workspaces into a new order.
  • Show as many workspaces as you like by dragging the bottom of the list.
  • Resume after a force quit. A terminal stopped from outside Tiri brings its agent back in its conversation.
  • A new agent starts in the workspace's folder, even with a past conversation open.
  • Smaller fixes. Paths with spaces open, folders outside the workspace open in Finder, and an agent working through a restart still shows as working.

0.3.1

9 November 2026

A Peek does everything a tab does, and fixes.

  • A Peek's name is a menu. Open it as a tab, to the side, below or in its own window, copy its path or reveal it. A right-click on its header does the same.
  • Files open in their own app. Choose kinds like .psd or .key to go straight there, and a page that wants a sign-in offers your browser.
  • An agent comes back whole after a crash or force quit, with its input box where it belongs.
  • Typing comes back after a click closes a Peek.
  • Lighter on the battery. A working agent's icon and a page with a ticking title no longer redraw the whole window.

0.3.0

8 November 2026

Run a file, or start your project, with one press.

  • ▶ Run above a file, or ⌃⌥N: Python, C++, Java and 17 more, in a real terminal. Input works, ↑ runs it again, and an error's path is a ⌘-click away.
  • ▶ Start in the toolbar keeps each workspace's dev servers and start scripts. Tiri suggests what your folders hold, opens the page beside your code, and your agent starts the same ones with tiri start, so there's never a second copy.
  • Titles and search with your own API key, without Apple Intelligence.
  • Past conversations read as conversations, and Codex titles are what was asked.
  • Files open in Tiri from Finder's Open With, tiri:// links and programs that want an editor.
  • Pictures pasted for an agent arrive as pictures.
  • Smaller fixes. The bar along the bottom stays put, dark pages stay dark, Connect to Server is easy to find, and language names show as they are.

0.2.1

7 November 2026

Fixes for restarting agents and for changing Tiri.

  • Restart starts clean. An agent that crashed or was stopped comes back without stray characters filling its input when the mouse moves.
  • Changing Tiri starts from the version you have. After an update, a new change no longer brings back the version before it.
  • One + in the explorer for New File, New Folder and Add Folder to Workspace.

0.2.0

7 November 2026

Faster terminals, and files from Finder.

  • Terminals draw on the graphics chip. Heavy output no longer holds up the rest of Tiri.
  • Files from Finder. Copy them in Finder and paste them on a folder in the explorer, or drop them on it, on your Mac or on a server. ⌘C in the explorer copies files back out.
  • ⌘-click any part of a long path, even one an agent wrapped over several lines.
  • ⌘-click finds files git ignores, like screenshots, logs and build output, and never opens another file on the same line.
  • Codex's first turn shows what it changed.
  • Several cursors: ⌘/, indent, and moving or deleting lines work at every cursor.
  • Smaller fixes. A deleted file no longer shows up in quick open. A ⌘-clicked folder shows what's inside. Review comments keep quotes and dashes as you typed them. Jumping to the end of a long diff is immediate.

0.1.0

6 November 2026

The first version for everyone.

  • Keep your agent. Claude Code, Codex, Grok or any command runs in its real terminal, with your config and subscription.
  • Files, diffs and pages are tabs beside the terminal, not in a drawer.
  • ⌘-click any path. The file floats over the session, and a path that doesn't exist as written still opens.
  • Every agent at a glance. The one that needs you is marked.
  • It improves itself. Ask, and your agent rewrites Tiri. Try it, then keep it or go back.
  • Your servers over your own SSH config.
  • Agents outlive the app. A quit, crash or force quit loses nothing, unsaved edits included.
  • Hard to stall. A huge paste, an agent rewriting an open file, a lid closed on an SSH session or a page that crashes stays in its own tab.
  • Themes for dark and light, or bring one from Cursor, VS Code or Ghostty.
  • A window per workspace, each with its own files and terminals.
  • English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
  • VoiceOver, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.
  • Collects nothing. Turn off update checks, and Tiri never goes online by itself.
